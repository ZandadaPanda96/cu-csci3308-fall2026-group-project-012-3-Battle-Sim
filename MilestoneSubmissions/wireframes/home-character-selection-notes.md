## Review notes

Fighter labels and move examples are illustrative, not an agreed roster or battle specification. The selected fighter is circled and labeled. Proposed move summaries are Quick hit (light damage), Heavy hit (more damage), and Guard (reduce next hit); the sketch explicitly marks them for team confirmation. The alternate no-selection state shows Start battle disabled until a fighter is picked; the selected state enables it.

Home includes the required title, welcome/player name, Play, and Log out. Team confirmation of the roster, moves, and added Home route, mobile layout, and an actual teammate/user walkthrough remain pending. These drafts do not establish that every criterion in #16 is complete.
